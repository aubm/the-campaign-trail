The adventure

Every adventure begins with a handful of strangers and a reason to take the next step. This is one of those stories.

Here, the ordinary gives way to the unknown. A rumor becomes a journey; a small kindness becomes an alliance. Before long, the road home is no longer the road we remember.

This is a sample adventure. Its setting, characters, and events are placeholders for a real campaign chronicle.
