# Art direction

A reader opens an illustrated fantasy journal: warm parchment, oxblood chapter titles, muted gold rules, generous serif type, and atmospheric landscape paintings. Images anchor each page; the reading surface stays quiet. No copied logos or official book artwork.

## First concept asset

Path: `public/images/the-long-road.webp`.
Generated with the built-in image generation tool. One shared concept asset is intentionally reused in the placeholder adventures and chapters. Generate story-specific images once real content is available.

Prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide hero illustration for a fantasy tabletop campaign journal website, original artwork evoking the painterly high-fantasy adventure illustration tradition of D&D fifth-edition books. Scene: a winding mountain trail leads from the foreground past ruined stone arches toward a distant medieval citadel rising above mist, a tiny party of three cloaked travelers on the trail gives scale. Dramatic warm amber dawn breaks through cool blue-gray clouds. Richly painted atmospheric depth, realistic proportions, visible oil-paint brushwork, muted earthy palette, cinematic composition, beautiful finely detailed environment with soft lost edges. Wide landscape framing. No typography, no lettering, no logos, no border, no watermark. This is an original fictional setting, not a reproduction of existing book artwork.

## Dawn Warrior Island

Path: `public/images/dawn-warrior-island.webp`.
Original AI-generated presentation illustration, distinct from the published adventure's cover. Generated with the built-in image tool and converted to WebP for the site.

Prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset: original wide landscape illustration for an author's D&D adventure showcase, not a product cover. Painterly high fantasy sourcebook illustration, visible oil paint brushwork, atmospheric realism and finely detailed vegetation. A deserted tropical island off the coast of Chult after a storm: turquoise sea, curving sandy beach, scattered driftwood and broken timbers from a wooden sailing ship in foreground, lush dense jungle climbing to a rocky plateau, a distant mainland silhouette across the sea. View from the edge of the beach toward the jungle and plateau. Clearing storm clouds, warm early sunlight breaking through, deep emerald greens and muted ochre with blue-gray atmosphere. Sense of discovery and vulnerability, beautiful but uncertain. No people, no monsters, no buildings, no text, no logos, no watermark. Wide 16:9 composition. No official D&D artwork reproduced.
